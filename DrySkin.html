Dry Skin

For dry skin types, it's essential to use ingredients that provide long-lasting hydration, nourishment, and protection to the skin.

Here are some effective skincare ingredients for dry skin types:
Hyaluronic acid: A natural humectant that attracts and retains moisture, leaving the skin feeling soft, supple, and hydrated.
Ceramides: Natural lipids that help repair and restore the skin's barrier function, retaining moisture and soothing dryness.
Glycerin: A natural humectant that helps retain moisture, soothe dryness, and support the skin's natural barrier function.
Niacinamide: A form of Vitamin B3 that improves skin elasticity, reduces inflammation, and enhances skin brightness.
Squalane: A natural moisturizer that hydrates and protects the skin, leaving it feeling soft, smooth, and supple.
Shea butter: A natural emollient that provides intense hydration, nourishment, and protection to dry skin.
Oat kernel oil: A natural anti-inflammatory that soothes and calms dry, irritated skin.


When using these ingredients, keep in mind:
Use rich, hydrating products that provide long-lasting moisture.
Avoid harsh exfoliants and opt for gentle, chemical-based exfoliants instead.
Apply products immediately after bathing or showering while the skin is still damp to help lock in moisture.
Consider using a humidifier to add moisture back into the air, especially during dry winter months.
Remember to patch test new skincare ingredients and products to ensure you don't have any sensitivity or allergic reactions.
